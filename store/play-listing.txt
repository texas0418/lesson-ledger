Lesson Ledger: Tutor Invoices

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars max)
Students, lessons, unbilled balances, and 2-tap invoices — built for tutors.

FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 chars max)
-----------------------------------------------------
You teach. You reschedule. You track who's paid, who hasn't, and whose parent needs a gentle nudge. Lesson Ledger takes everything after the teaching off your plate.

BUILT FOR HOW TUTORING ACTUALLY WORKS
• Students with their rates, subjects, and parents' contact details
• A weekly schedule that logs lessons as they happen
• Every logged lesson accrues to an unbilled balance per student — you always know exactly who owes what

INVOICES IN TWO TAPS
Turn a student's unbilled lessons into a clean, professional PDF invoice and send it through any app you like. Dates, lessons, amounts, totals — done before your next student rings the doorbell.

POLITE REMINDERS THAT WORK
When an invoice sits unpaid, Lesson Ledger drafts the courteous follow-up message for you — timed right, worded professionally, sent from your own account with one tap. No more composing awkward texts to parents at 10 pm.

YOUR BUSINESS STAYS YOURS
Everything lives on your phone: no account, no cloud, no analytics. Students' information never leaves your device unless you send it. Export a backup any time — backups are free forever.

FIRST STUDENT FREE, FOREVER
Every feature included with your first student, free, for as long as you like. Lesson Ledger Pro ($4.99/month or $49.99/year) unlocks unlimited students when your roster grows.

Private tutors, music teachers, coaches, test-prep instructors — if you invoice by the lesson, this ledger was ruled for you.
