Lesson Ledger

Privacy policy & support

The short version: Lesson Ledger keeps your data on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no servers of ours. The only data that ever leaves your phone is what you send yourself — invoices and payment reminders through apps you choose, backups you export, and purchases processed by the app store.

What Lesson Ledger stores

Your students, schedule, logged lessons, payments, invoices, and settings are stored in a local database on your device. We cannot see, access, or recover this data — it never reaches us. Deleting the app deletes the data, so export a backup first if you want to keep it.

Invoices and reminders

Lesson Ledger builds invoice PDFs and drafts payment-reminder messages for you, but it never sends anything itself. When you share an invoice or a reminder, it is handed to the app you choose, and sending happens from your own accounts under your control.

Purchases

Lesson Ledger Pro is an auto-renewing subscription purchased through the app store on your device (Apple App Store or Google Play). Purchases are processed by the store and validated by RevenueCat, our purchase-management provider, using an anonymous identifier and your store receipt. This does not include your name, email address, students, or any content from the app. Subscriptions renew unless cancelled in your store account settings; your first student stays free forever either way.

Backups

Backups are plain files you export and keep wherever you like. They are yours; nothing is uploaded anywhere by the app.

Children

Lesson Ledger is a business tool for tutors and is not directed at children. Student records you keep in the app stay on your device, as above.
